Privacy Policy for X-Player

Last updated: June 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how X-Player handles information when you use the X-Player Android TV application. X-Player is a media player that lets you add your own playlists or play media from local storage.

Information We Collect

X-Player does not require you to create an account with us. The app may store information that you enter or generate while using it, including:

  • Playlist names, playlist URLs, and related details that you add.
  • Profile names and app preferences.
  • Favorites, watch history, and continue-watching progress.
  • Local media metadata needed to display and play your media library.

How Information Is Used

Information stored by X-Player is used to provide app features such as loading playlists, playing your media, remembering favorites, resuming playback, and maintaining app settings.

Data Storage

X-Player stores app data locally on your device. Playlist details may be protected using Android security features where available. We do not operate a separate X-Player account service for collecting or syncing your personal data.

Network Requests

When you add a playlist URL or media source, X-Player may connect to the locations you provide in order to load content lists, retrieve artwork, and play media. Those external locations may receive technical information such as your IP address, request details, device information, and media requests according to their own privacy practices.

Third-Party Content and Services

X-Player does not provide, host, sell, or verify media content or playlists. You are responsible for the playlists, files, and media sources you add to the app. External media locations and services are independent from X-Player.

Analytics and Advertising

X-Player does not include third-party advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs unless this policy is updated to describe them. If future versions add analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or similar services, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when those features are released.

Children's Privacy

X-Player is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

You can remove playlists, profiles, favorites, watch history, and other app data from within the app where supported, or by clearing the app's storage through Android system settings. Removing the app from your device may also remove locally stored app data.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures available on Android to protect locally stored data. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You should only add playlist and media details that you are authorized to use.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be identified by a new "Last updated" date. Your continued use of X-Player after an update means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the developer contact information listed for X-Player on Google Play.
